# Example prompts / Prompts de ejemplo

Prompts that work well with the **Ourway – Camino de Santiago** connector, grouped by intent.
The assistant should call `camino_de_santiago_info` for questions and the planning tools for trips.

## Questions (free, instant)

- Do I need a pilgrim credential? Where do I get it?
- How does the Compostela work? Where does the 100 km rule start on the Portuguese Way?
- What is the best month to walk the Camino Francés?
- Which Camino should I pick for a first time? I have 7 days.
- How many days from Porto to Santiago on the Coastal Way?
- Is the Camino safe for a solo female traveller?
- Can I bring my dog on the Camino?
- ¿Qué llevo en la mochila para el Camino del Norte en octubre?
- ¿Cuánto cuesta hacer el Camino desde Sarria en hoteles?

## Plan a trip

- I want to walk the Camino de Santiago from Sarria the first week of September, 2 people. Organize it for me.
- Plan a 7-night Portuguese Camino from Tui for a family with two kids (8 and 11), breakfast included, luggage transfer.
- We are four friends, experienced hikers, 10 days in May from Oviedo on the Primitivo. Country houses if possible.
- Quiero hacer el Camino Inglés desde Ferrol en 5 etapas a ritmo tranquilo, habitación individual con baño.
- Proponme el Camino Francés desde León en bici, 6 noches, presupuesto 800 € por persona.

## Adjust a quote

- Make the package cheaper without changing the itinerary.
- Upgrade the hotels where possible.
- I want a more central hotel on night 3.
- Add breakfast on the nights that don't include it.
- Show me alternatives for the hotel in Portomarín.

## Book & follow up

- I approve the itinerary and the total. Create my proposal — here are my details: …
- How is my proposal PET-123 doing?
- My proposal expired; can you re-quote the same trip?
